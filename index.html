    Build the concept

    Start from why the rule exists.

    Why an accrual is recorded at all. Why a firm holds inventory. Why a price ceiling creates a shortage. The mechanics follow once the logic is in place — nothing is memorised before it is understood.

    Move straight into unfamiliar cases.

    Worked examples give way to new scenarios, because that is where the marks are. Command words are taught as deliberately as content: state, explain, analyse, evaluate.

    Then the clock, and the mark scheme.

    Timed past papers marked against the official scheme, session by session, until the structure of an answer is automatic and time stops being the enemy.

    On campus in Karachi, and live online.

    Separate bands for O Level / IGCSE, AS and A2, so no session is diluted across levels. Online means live — same board, same questions, same pace.
