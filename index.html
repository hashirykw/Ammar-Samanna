The method

Concept. Application. Paper.

Nothing is memorised before it is understood — and nothing counts as learnt until it survives a real Cambridge question under time.

01

Build the concept

Every topic starts from why the rule exists — why an accrual is recorded, why a firm holds inventory, why a price ceiling creates a shortage.

02

Apply it in context

Worked examples give way to unfamiliar cases. Command words — state, explain, analyse, evaluate — are taught as carefully as content.

03

Drill the paper

Timed past papers marked against the official scheme, session by session, until the shape of a full-mark answer is automatic.
